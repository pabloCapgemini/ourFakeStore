# Slide 2 — Audience: Review Comments

## What's still accurate
- The four delivery roles listed remain the primary audience
- C&CA framing is still valid

## Possible additions to discuss

### Product Owners / Product Managers
- Proficient AI-assisted teams consume backlogs faster than orgs can make decisions to feed them (reinforced strongly in H2 2025)
- If this presentation is meant to drive action, POs need to be in the room — the bottleneck has shifted upstream to product decision-making

### Broader non-engineering roles
- H2 2025 has seen the emergence of AI-first teams where the primary task is *not* coding — e.g. data, content, operations, business analysis workflows
- The patterns observed (agentic delegation, human-in-the-loop, context engineering) are proving applicable far beyond software delivery
- **Decision needed**: Do we widen the audience scope to acknowledge this, or keep the presentation focused on C&CA software engineering delivery roles?

## Recommendation
Add Product Owners to the list. Add a footnote or callout acknowledging that the patterns discussed are relevant beyond software engineering — without making the slide feel unfocused.
