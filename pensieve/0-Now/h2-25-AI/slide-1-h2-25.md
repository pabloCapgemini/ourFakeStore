# Introduction

This presentation captures key observations and learnings from the GABL C&CA Agentic Software Engineering Bootstrap Team's current research into AI adoption in software engineering.

It explores how modern development practices, team composition, and internal platforms are evolving to support agentic automation. The deck **highlights the importance of clear objectives, robust design, and disciplined processes** when working with AI agents.

It also discusses the shifting roles of humans and AI in task execution, the **impact on team dynamics and competencies**, and the **critical need for mature developer tooling and automation**.

It aims to provide practical insights for successfully integrating AI into software engineering to drive productivity, quality, and innovation.
