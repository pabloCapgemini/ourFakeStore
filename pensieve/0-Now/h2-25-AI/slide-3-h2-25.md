# Modern Software Engineering

## Internal Developer Platform *(2017 onwards)*

Internal Developer Platform features are required to support the high standard of build and test automation needed to support agentic development.

## Squad with coding assistants *(2021 onwards)*

Inexperienced developers are more productive but need more training & support, as they can become dependent on AI to code, rather than learning on the job.

## AI Agents and Agentic Software Engineering *(2025 Q2 onwards)*

Team composition critical: expertise in software engineering is essential to craft prompts and processes and then to understand what AI is producing.
