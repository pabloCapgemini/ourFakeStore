# What have we learned?

> **Legend:** `[S&C]` Skills & Competency · `[CE]` Context Engineering · `[WoW]` Ways of Working · `[AUTO]` Automation

| Planning | Design | Agile | Development | Maintenance |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Clear objectives, use-cases, requirements and success criteria are even more critical `[S&C]` | Detailed software architecture, design and specification is more important than ever before `[CE]` | Teams change their ways of working as they accelerate when using AI tools `[WoW]` | Vibe coding only works for experts that work alone, everything else needs process `[S&C]` | AI agents can and should automate software patching and maintenance `[AUTO]` |
| Team composition matters: expertise in software engineering is essential `[S&C]` | Design of the development process, prompts, AI agent & tool configuration is highly influential `[CE]` | As proficiency grows task size increases, reduced decomposition into granular tasks `[WoW]` | Human-in-the-loop reviews of code are a bottleneck that impedes productivity `[S&C]` | Near zero technical debt is a real prospect and will unlock new value streams `[AUTO]` |
| Discussions about requirements and the solution must be accessible to AI `[CE]` | Domain Driven Design is a practical & useful approach to designing with and for AI `[CE]` | User Stories are the wrong abstraction for coding with agents (insufficient context) `[WoW]` | Comprehensive build and test automation is essential `[AUTO]` | Comprehensive and robust observability is essential `[AUTO]` |
