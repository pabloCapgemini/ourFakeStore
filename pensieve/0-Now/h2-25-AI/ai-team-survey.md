# AI Team Survey

A short survey to gather team perspectives on AI adoption in software engineering.
Please answer honestly — there are no right or wrong answers. Aim for 5–10 minutes total.

---

## Section 1: Your Context

1. What is your primary role? *(Architect / BA / Delivery Manager / Software Engineer / Product Owner / Other)*
2. How long have you been using AI tools in your day-to-day work? *(< 3 months / 3–6 months / 6–12 months / 1+ year)*

---

## Section 2: Ways of Working

3. Are you using AI tools beyond software development tasks (e.g. analysis, content, operations, business processes)? *(Yes, regularly / Yes, occasionally / No, only for coding / No, not using AI tools)*
4. When working with AI agents, how often do software engineering fundamentals (e.g. clean design, modularity, testability) directly affect the quality of AI outputs? *(Rarely / Sometimes / Often / Almost always)*
5. Have you worked in or observed agent-to-agent workflows (where one AI agent orchestrates or delegates to others)? *(Yes, regularly / Yes, occasionally / Not yet but aware of it / No)*

---

## Section 3: What We've Learned

6. When you direct an AI agent on a task, how often do you feel you can clearly articulate the context it needs? *(Rarely / Sometimes / Often / Almost always)*
7. Have you noticed a difference in AI effectiveness between engineers on your team that is **not** explained by seniority or experience level? *(Yes, clearly / Somewhat / Not really / Haven't observed this)*
8. Has your team changed how you write requirements or task descriptions to better work with AI agents? *(Yes, significantly / Yes, somewhat / In progress / Not yet)*
9. How would you describe the impact of AI-assisted development on technical debt in your codebase? *(Significantly reduced / Slightly reduced / No change / Slightly increased / Significantly increased)*
10. Has human code review changed in your team since adopting AI? *(Yes — less line-level, more architectural / Yes — AI now does initial review pass / Minimal change / No change)*

---

## Section 4: Your Role in the AI Spectrum

11. Where would you place your team's typical AI usage today? *(AI-assisted code completion / Agent-mode partial delegation / Agentic tools with significant delegation / Task-assignable agents / Fully autonomous multi-agent systems)*
12. In the last 6 months, has your role shifted more towards directing and governing AI rather than directly executing tasks? *(Yes, significantly / Yes, somewhat / Minimal change / No change)*
13. What is your biggest personal challenge when working at higher levels of AI delegation? *(Trusting the output / Evaluating quality / Defining objectives clearly / Losing touch with the detail / Other)*

---

---

## Section 5: Team & Organisational Impact

14. Has the speed of your team's delivery created pressure upstream — e.g. on product decisions, prioritisation, or organisational governance? *(Yes, significantly / Yes, somewhat / Not yet / No)*
15. In your team, has the focus shifted from writing code to designing processes, reviewing outputs, and making architectural decisions? *(Yes, significantly / Yes, somewhat / Minimal change / No)*

---

## Section 6: Foundations for Success

16. Which of the following does your team currently have in place? *(Select all that apply: Mature CI/CD pipeline / Comprehensive automated test suite / Documented architecture decisions (ADRs) / A defined process for working with AI agents / None of the above)*
17. Where do your team's requirements, specs, and technical decisions primarily live? *(Jira/Confluence or similar / Shared docs (Google Docs, Notion, etc.) / Markdown files in the repo / Mix of the above / No consistent standard)*
18. Does your team have shared, standardised AI agent configurations (custom skills, commands, context rules) that everyone uses? *(Yes, fully standardised / Yes, partially / We're working on it / No, everyone configures independently)*
