# Notes: Apex Gaps & Concerns — March 3, 2026

- Apex does not support Iteration (capital I), which is a core pattern of agentic success in enterprise SDLC — single-click artifact generation ignores that refinement through iteration is essential, especially for coding.
- The document's stated outcome of 4x productivity improvement at scale should be softened to "significant productivity improvements" (greater than 20%) without committing to a specific multiplier.
- Apex is a workflow contract, not a feature set — treating it as a feature backlog leads to feature envy and insufficient user feedback, and it should instead be managed as a product program using standard Product Management techniques.
- Apex's heavy reliance on centralized RAG (ingesting hundreds of client repos via a service called Vegas with a central Gemini loop) is a risk, given that agentic search (as adopted by Claude Code) is local, distributed, and currently cheaper — with LLM-native companies subsidizing tokens and winning this space.
- Apex needs to work "in the wild" — adoption by engineering teams must be low-friction and require minimal onboarding or learning curve.
