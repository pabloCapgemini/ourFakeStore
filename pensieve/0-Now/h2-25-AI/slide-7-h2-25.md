# Successful Adoption of AI in Software Engineering Requires:

| **Information Architecture & Context Engineering** | **Internal Developer Platform & Test Automation** | **Disciplined Adoption of a Well-defined AI-Development Model** |
|---|---|---|
| ✅ Proficient teams consume the backlog faster which puts pressure on organisations ability to make decisions that feed the backlog | ✅ Developer tooling and automation must be mature, well-maintained and systematically integrated into all workflows that lead to production. | ✅ Development teams must be consistent and disciplined in adopting a well-defined process for collaborating with AI Agents. |
